3. **Decision-Making**: Each agent decides what to do based on its own information and the information it receives from other agents. It's like how in a game, each player might make decisions based on the information they have about the game state.

4. **Interaction**: Agents can interact with each other in many ways. They can cooperate to solve problems, compete for resources, or even deceive each other, much like different teams in a game might interact.

5. **Flexibility**: MAS are designed to be flexible and adaptable. They can be used to simulate various real-world systems such as logistics networks, urban planning, and even social networks.
